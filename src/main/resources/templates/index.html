The Vehicle

Built for
the wild

Every safari runs in an open-top 4×4 from our managed fleet, inspected, scheduled and dispatched through the same system you book with.

  1. Canvas sun canopyShade overhead, open sides for photography.
  2. Raised tiered seatingEvery row sees over the one in front.
  3. All-terrain 4×4Built for red-earth tracks and river crossings.
  4. Maintained fleetService records tracked; vehicles under repair are never dispatched.

The Safari Experience

Every detail, in the field.

01

The Vehicle

Modified open-top 4×4s with raised seating, maintained and dispatched by our fleet team so every seat has a clear view.

02

The Guide

Certified, multilingual naturalist trackers who read the forest, keep a respectful distance and know where the animals move.

03

The Journey

Morning, afternoon and full-day drives scheduled around park hours, with seat capacity tracked for every departure.

04

The Wild

Leopards, elephant herds, sloth bears and hundreds of bird species, seen on their terms, in their habitat.
